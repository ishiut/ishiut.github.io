## Fermat's Last Theorem

Anthropic announced that Claude produced the first end-to-end, computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem (See [Formalizing Fermat's Last Theorem](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)). 

This proof was formalized in Lean and is based on the simplified proof by Darmon, Diamond, and Taylor.
Simplified, yet using the same framework as Wiles's proof, and very complicated. 
It is remarkable that AI almost autonomously proved a theorem of this magnitude. 

## The Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem

Another major announcement came from OpenAI today: Their AI system solved the Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem, one of the Millennium Prize Problems. 
See [https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/). 
I know virtually nothing about this problem, but the page says "an initially smooth fluid at rest can develop a singularity in a finite time". 
This appears to be a really new theorem proved by AI. 
Well, it just came out so probably we should wait until knowledgeable people read the proof. 
However, if it holds up under peer review, it is one of the most significant breakthroughs by AI to date.
